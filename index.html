Regional Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

SQL (PostgreSQL) Python (Pandas) Power BI
Business Context

A retail business had 5 years of US sales data but lacked clarity on which regions, products, and channels were driving profitable growth versus creating risk.

Key Questions
  • Which regions and states contribute most to revenue and profit?
  • Is revenue concentrated in a small set of products or customers?
  • Which channels maximize volume vs. profitability?
  • Is pricing or volume the primary profit driver?
Key Insights
  • 64,000+ transactions revealed strong revenue concentration across a small number of SKUs.
  • Pricing showed the strongest correlation with profit and revenue, outperforming volume.
  • Certain regions generated high revenue but weaker margins, indicating cost or pricing issues.
  • Clear seasonality patterns impacted revenue stability and planning.
Business Impact
  • Highlighted pricing as the primary lever for profit improvement.
  • Identified SKU, customer, and geographic concentration risks.
  • Enabled data-driven decisions for pricing, channel strategy, and regional investment.

Customer Shopping Behavior & Segmentation Analysis

SQL (PostgreSQL) Python (Pandas) Power BI
Business Context

An e-commerce business needed to better understand customer behavior to improve retention, target high-value customers, and optimize promotional strategy.

Key Questions
  • Who are the highest-value customers?
  • How does purchase frequency impact revenue?
  • Do shipping choices and subscriptions affect spending?
Key Insights
  • Repeat and loyal customers were the primary contributors to total revenue.
  • Express shipping users showed higher average purchase values.
  • Subscribers consistently spent more than non-subscribers.
Business Impact
  • Supported targeted promotions for high-value customer segments.
  • Informed subscription and shipping-based pricing strategies.
  • Improved understanding of customer lifetime value drivers.
