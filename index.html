Regional Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

SQL (PostgreSQL) Python (Pandas) Power BI
Business Context

A retail business had 5 years of historical US sales data but lacked clarity on which regions, products, and sales channels were driving profitable growth versus creating revenue and margin risk.

Key Business Questions
  • Which regions and states contribute most to revenue and profit?
  • Is revenue concentrated in a small set of products or customers?
  • Which channels maximize volume vs. profitability?
  • Is pricing or volume the primary driver of profit?
Key Insights
  • Analysis of 64,000+ transactions revealed strong revenue concentration across a small number of SKUs, creating dependency risk.
  • Pricing showed the strongest correlation with both revenue and profit, outperforming volume-based strategies.
  • Certain regions generated high revenue but weaker margins, indicating pricing or cost inefficiencies.
Business Impact
  • Identified pricing as the primary lever for profit improvement.
  • Highlighted SKU, customer, and geographic concentration risks.
  • Enabled data-driven decisions for pricing strategy, channel focus, and regional investment.

Customer Shopping Behavior & Segmentation Analysis

SQL (PostgreSQL) Python (Pandas) Power BI
Business Context

An e-commerce business needed to better understand customer purchasing behavior to improve retention, identify high-value customers, and optimize promotional and pricing strategies.

Key Business Questions
  • Who are the highest-value customers?
  • How does purchase frequency impact revenue?
  • Do shipping preferences and subscriptions affect spending behavior?
Key Insights
  • Repeat and loyal customers were the primary contributors to total revenue.
  • Express shipping users showed higher average purchase values.
  • Subscribers consistently spent more than non-subscribers.
Business Impact
  • Supported targeted promotions for high-value customer segments.
  • Informed subscription-based and shipping-based pricing strategies.
  • Improved understanding of customer lifetime value drivers.
